# The Storytelling Studio's crown jewel 

My team at Gannett was responsible for designing, engineering and maintaining the In-Depth article framework. This collection of tools enables more than 100 web producers across the company to craft visually stunning, reader-focused multimedia presentations using the everyday CMS and our own story editor.

We started the project because the existing story template at the time was plain, restrictive, and the only option. This meant that a gameday recap carried the same perceptual importance as a Pultizer Prize contender. To rectify this, we built a full-featured story editor, replacing the conventional story rendering layer.

With it, we empowered digital designers to have complete control over an article's presentation: the image layouts, videos, color palette, and nearly every other facet of the look-and-feel. We built more than a dozen interactive tools for this framework and even added ways to include custom interactives.

It was used to create more than 7,500 articles (as of December 2022) and was the tool of choice to present the strongest, most impactful journalism Gannett has to offer. It generated hundreds of thousands of pageviews, a 2x increase in engaged time over standard templates, and had a high subscriber conversion rate.

_Unfortunately, Gannett decided to archive all of these great visual works of journalism, reverting them all to standard page templates. The links presented here are to preserved instances hosted elsewhere._
